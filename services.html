Common questions

What counts as a critical SLA incident?

Anything that stops a person from working entirely: a failed router, a dead laptop, or a lost VPN gateway. These are covered by our four-hour replacement commitment on the Managed and Enterprise GCC tiers.

Do you deploy across multiple cities?

Yes. Deployments are coordinated centrally and carried out through regional partners, so a GCC rollout can span several cities on a single asset register and a single SLA.

Can data analytics run without the infrastructure work?

Yes. Many clients engage us for dashboards and analysis alone, using their existing data sources. Infrastructure and analytics are scoped and billed separately.

Which visualization tools do you build in?

We work in the tools your team already uses, commonly Power BI or Tableau, and can deliver lightweight custom web dashboards where that fits better.

Can you help us set up a new GCC from scratch?

Yes. Our GCC advisory covers the full path, from operating model and investment plan through HR, Finance, IT, and Administration readiness to governance and growth.

Who owns the hardware you procure?

You do. We procure, configure, and asset-tag on your behalf, and every device is registered to your organization in the ITAM register from the day it is deployed.

Not sure which tier fits?

Send us your team size and current setup. We will recommend a scope and return a plan within one business day.
