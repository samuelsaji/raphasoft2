How a deployment runs

The same four steps govern a single home office fit-out and a multi-site GCC rollout. Only the scale changes.

Assess

We survey the network, the space, and existing hardware against your corporate policy.

Design

Network topology, ergonomic layout, and the full asset list are specified and approved.

Deploy

Hardware is procured, configured, tagged, and installed on site or shipped ready to use.

Operate

Monitoring and SLA-backed support take over from the first day of go-live.
